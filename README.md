# 🚗 DrivePrep — California Driving Test Practice

A free, no-signup study app for the **California DMV knowledge test and behind-the-wheel road test**. Everything runs in your browser from a single page — nothing to install, no account needed, and your progress is saved on your device.

**👉 Try it now: [maadhavkothari.github.io/Ca-driving-test-prep](https://maadhavkothari.github.io/Ca-driving-test-prep/)**

Works great on your phone — open the link and add it to your home screen.

## What's inside

### 📖 Theory practice
- **208 practice questions** with instant feedback and plain-English explanations for every answer
- Topics: right of way, speed & lanes, parking, alcohol & law, safety & emergencies, sharing the road, signs & signals, official DMV-style questions, and CA handbook drills
- **36-question mock exam** that mirrors the real test format, with a pass/fail score and a review of everything you missed

### 🛑 Road signs
- **35 US road signs** drawn right in the app
- Study them in a grid, drill with **flashcards**, or test yourself with a sign quiz

### 🚗 Road test prep
- Step-by-step walkthroughs of **8 key maneuvers**: parallel parking, three-point turns, lane changes (SMOG), unprotected left turns, hill parking, and more
- A **pre-drive checklist** covering what the examiner asks before you leave the lot
- A list of the **most common automatic fails** so you know what to avoid

### 📊 Progress tracking
- Per-topic mastery stars, streaks, and score history
- Saved automatically in your browser (localStorage) — no login, and your data never leaves your device
- Light and dark mode, following your system setting

## Running it locally

It's one HTML file with zero dependencies. Either:

- Download [`index.html`](index.html) and double-click it, or
- Clone the repo and open the file:

```sh
git clone https://github.com/MaadhavKothari/Ca-driving-test-prep.git
open Ca-driving-test-prep/index.html
```

## Disclaimer

This is an unofficial study aid. Always check the latest [California Driver's Handbook](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/handbook/california-driver-handbook/) — rules and test formats can change.

Good luck on your test! 🎉
